# Our Love Story 💕 | Romantic Scrapbook Website

A romantic couple memories scrapbook web application handcrafted for your girlfriend. Designed with a cute pastel aesthetic, washi tapes, tilted polaroids, animated hearts, a live relationship counter starting from **August 20**, timeline photo alignment controls, an interactive 3D wax seal love letter, secret surprise chamber with redeemable love coupons, and background music player.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **🏠 Home & Welcome Screen**:
   - Cute pastel scrapbook banner with washi tape, stamps, and your **outdoor couple selfie**!
   - Customizable couple names.
   - **Live Relationship Counter (August 20 to Live Time)**:
     - Counts live **Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds** since August 20.
     - Pulsing red **LIVE** indicator and real-time clock updating every single second!
   - "Send a Kiss 💋" button with celebratory kiss animation and sweet sound chimes.
   - Ambient floating pastel hearts and sparkles canvas.

2. **⏳ Our Romantic Timeline with Photo Alignment Controls**:
   - Scrapbook twine rope with wooden clothespin cards featuring your **real couple memories and photos**!
   - **Photo Alignment Options for Each Photo**:
     - ⬅️ **Left**: Pin photo card to the left side of the rope.
     - 🎯 **Center**: Expand photo card across the twine in a centered scrapbook layout.
     - ➡️ **Right**: Pin photo card to the right side of the rope.
     - Each card has an inline `[ ⬅️ Left | 🎯 Center | ➡️ Right ]` button bar that lets you change its alignment in 1 click!
   - **Global Layout Switcher**:
     - 🔀 *Zig-Zag* (Alternating)
     - ⬅️ *All Left*
     - 🎯 *All Center*
     - ➡️ *All Right*
   - Milestone stories: *Day One (August 20), Bus Journeys, Mall Dates, Station Canopy, Bike Trips, Cafe Snacks, Cheek Kiss, and Traditional Saree*.
   - Category filter pills (*All, Milestones, Dates, Adventures, Inside Jokes*).
   - "➕ Pin a New Memory" button with custom alignment selector.

3. **📷 Polaroid Gallery ("Frozen in Time")**:
   - Decorated corkboard displaying **all 11 couple photos**!
   - Realistic Polaroid frames with soft drop shadows, washi tapes (pink, cream, mint, gold, lavender), and tilt angles.
   - Interactive heart like counters on each photo.
   - Lightbox zoom modal when clicking any polaroid.
   - "📸 Pin a New Polaroid Photo" to add more photos directly from your phone/computer or image URLs.

4. **💌 3D Wax Seal Love Letter**:
   - Realistic vintage envelope with a heart-stamped burgundy wax seal.
   - Interactive wax seal breaking animation: top flap opens, seal cracks, and the parchment letter slides out smoothly.
   - Aged paper texture, folded creases, elegant calligraphy, and personal romantic letter honoring August 20.
   - "✏️ Edit My Words" button to rewrite or personalize your letter right inside the browser.

5. **🎁 Secret Surprise Chamber**:
   - Mystery gift box wrapped in ribbons with glowing heart.
   - "Tap to Reveal the Surprise! 🎁" button triggers a colorful burst of heart fireworks and confetti.
   - Unveils your secret love message and a sweet framed couple photo!
   - **6 Romantic "Anytime" Love Coupons**:
     - *Unlimited Warm Bear Hugs (Never expires)*
     - *Midnight Ice Cream & Snack Run*
     - *Movie Night - You Pick Whatever You Want*
     - *Relaxing Head & Back Massage*
     - *Home-Cooked Dinner Date*
     - *Spontaneous Bike & Road Trip*
   - Each coupon can be clicked to be stamped: `REDEEMED WITH LOVE ❤️` (persists on reload!).

6. **🎵 Background Music Player**:
   - Floating vintage vinyl record player widget that spins when music plays.
   - Play/pause toggle, volume slider, and track title display.
   - Dual audio engine:
     - Plays any romantic MP3 audio track or uploaded song.
     - Built-in **Web Audio API Romantic Music Box synthesizer fallback** that generates a calming lullaby melody completely offline with zero loading issues!
   - Polite browser autoplay policy banner at the top so audio starts smoothly upon first tap.

## 🚀 How to Open & View Locally

### Local Server Link:
👉 **[http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)**

*(If running python server: `python -m http.server 8080`)*

### Direct File:
You can also simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox without any server.

## 💖 How to Personalize

1. **In-App "✏️ Personalize" Button**:
   - Click **"✏️ Personalize"** in the top navigation bar.
   - Adjust girlfriend's name, boyfriend's name, start date (August 20), quotes, or custom MP3 song.
   - Saves automatically to `localStorage`.
2. **Share with Girlfriend**:
   - Click **"💾 Save & Share Scrapbook"** in the footer to copy a personalized link (e.g. `?gf=Sophia&bf=Alex&date=2024-08-20`).
3. **Change Photo Alignment**:
   - On the timeline, click **Left ⬅️**, **Center 🎯**, or **Right ➡️** on any card to reposition that specific photo card immediately!
